Payment

You pay by mobile money before we deliver.

We deliver across Kampala. The delivery fee starts from UGX 10,000.

A sample set is three scents of your choice, 2 ml each. It costs UGX 10,000. Delivery in Kampala is included.

Product safety

For external use only. Our perfumes contain alcohol and fragrance. Do a patch test on a small area of skin first. Keep away from flames, eyes and children.

If something is wrong with your order, message us on WhatsApp. We will help put it right.
