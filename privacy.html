Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

ENZIWA is an artisan perfume house in Kampala, Uganda. This page explains what happens to your information when you use our website and order from us.

What we collect

Our website has no accounts, no checkout, no forms, no analytics and no cookies of its own.

We only get what you choose to send us on WhatsApp. That can be your name, your phone number, your delivery address and your order details.

Why we use it

We use it to take your order, deliver it and follow up with you about it.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep it only as long as we need it for orders and our records.

Google Fonts

Our pages load the Jost font from Google Fonts. When the font loads, Google receives your IP address and some details about your browser. Google uses this under its own privacy policy.

WhatsApp

You order by tapping a button that opens WhatsApp with a message already written. The message goes to our business number, +256 773 344075. WhatsApp is run by Meta. Meta handles your messages under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you. You can also ask us to delete it. Message us on WhatsApp and we will help.

The law

Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 applies.
